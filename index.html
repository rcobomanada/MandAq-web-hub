Methodology & Research Integrity

Standards, verification methods, and data taxonomy utilized across M&A Deal Hub.

1. Transaction Qualification Standards

Transactions enter this repository upon execution of definitive statutory merger agreements, stock purchases exceeding 50% voting equity, or critical asset consolidations. Speculative rumors and unconfirmed bids are excluded.

2. Currency & Nominal Disclosed Valuations

All values represent disclosed Transaction/Enterprise Values at announcement converted to USD where applicable. Non-disclosed private transactions are preserved as Undisclosed and explicitly omitted from denominator averages to avoid skewing.

3. Termination & Regulatory Intervention

Deals dissolved due to DOJ/FTC antitrust challenges, CMA (UK) blocks, European Commission interventions, or mutual commercial walkaways are categorized as Terminated to reflect market friction accurately.

Institutional Disclaimer

M&A Deal Hub is an academic, data-driven financial research terminal. Information is synthesized from SEC 8-K filings, corporate press releases, and global financial disclosures. It does not constitute investment advice or securities recommendations.
